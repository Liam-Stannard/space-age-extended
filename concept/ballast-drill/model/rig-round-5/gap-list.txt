the rounded square 1.94 out, corner arcs 0.44). A gap is a cell with something over the band's
roof (Z1 1.25) and open air under it at the ledge (Z0 + 0.06), the band's middle or its roof
(Z1 - 0.04). Counted on the signed-off model (no cut-back), T03f's cast plate (before) and
T03g's (after); a cell open before or after but not in the signed-off model is one the
cut-back made.
