Vent Pump round 3: the helium line's family colour (written by artist A, 2026-10-12; first to write decides)

Model (the lagged helium line in every concept):
  lagging canvas, painted grey-violet:   #7E7488  (base)  over metal #5A5260
  identification bands (every ~0.22 t):  #B6A6C8  pale violet band, 0.05 t wide,
                                         with a thin #3A3440 edge each side
  flanges, clamps, valves on the line:   bare steel (the kit's "bare"); no frost, no blue
  helium kit (meter body, bubbler, gauges): brass cases, as the kit's gauge()

Why not helium-3's own base_color (fluids.lua: r .55 g .78 b .90): the brief bans pale blue
and cold colour in the model; grey-violet keeps clear of the melt's orange, the oxide red,
the teal and the green paints.

Path overlay (2x sheets):
  helium in:           #B48CFF  (violet)
  molten kamacite:     #FF8030  (orange)
  spent / recovered gas: #4CD6C0  (teal-green)
  line width 3 px at 2x, arrows every ~40 px, numbered nodes as round 2
