# The Core's production line

**Status:** two steps are built: crushing ore, and making plate. The rest is not
designed yet. It depends on how the game is won.

## 1. Crushing

The Heavy crusher turns 4 kamacite ore into 6 crushed kamacite and 2 kamacite
fines in 4 s. Where it can be built: its spec.

## 2. Plate

A furnace turns 3 crushed kamacite into 1 kamacite plate in 6.4 s, and 4
kamacite fines into 1 plate in 9.6 s. Ore can't be smelted until it is
crushed. Nothing uses the plate yet (TODO.md, "Decide what kamacite plate is
for").
