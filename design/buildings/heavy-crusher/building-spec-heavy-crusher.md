# Heavy crusher — building spec

Heavy crusher. Crushes kamacite ore, wherever there is gravity.

---

# 1. Facts

- **Name:** Heavy crusher
- **What it does:** crushes kamacite ore into crushed kamacite and
  kamacite fines. Its fines are what opens Vent tapping.
- **Size and directions:** 2 × 3, 4 directions.
- **Where it can be built:** where gravity is 1 or more: on any planet,
  not on a space platform.
- **Power:** 810 kW electric. It also needs 100 kW of heating, so on
  Aquilo it freezes unless it is heated.
- **Inputs and outputs:** one item in, kamacite ore; two out, crushed
  kamacite and kamacite fines. No fluid connections.
- **Speed:** crafting speed 1; 2 module slots.
- **Cost:** 30 low density structures, 15 steel plate, 15 electric
  engine units; 15 s.
- **Weight:** 100 kg, stack of 10.
- **Unlocked by:** Kamacite processing.
- **Art:** vanilla's crusher's, unchanged (Liam, 2026-11-16); not a
  placeholder.
- **Remnants and explosion:** Complete: vanilla's crusher remnants and
  electric furnace explosion.
- **Art files:** none of its own; it draws vanilla's crusher
  (`__space-age__/graphics/entity/crusher/`).
