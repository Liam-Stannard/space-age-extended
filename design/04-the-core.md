# The Core

The mod's destination: a planet past the Shattered Planet, reached down a
corridor flown on space platforms. How the game is won: see `00-vision.md`.

## 1. What the Core is

The intact metallic heart of the destroyed world: a frozen crust over a
still-hot iron-nickel interior, with an atmosphere of its own. It is the
densest body in the system.

### Surface properties

- **Pressure: 200.** See below.
- **Gravity: 50.** The heaviest world in the game. Everything that needs
  gravity works: chests, rails, vehicles and the cargo landing pad.
- **Magnetic field: 0.** A dead dynamo. The electromagnetic plant and the
  recycler can't be made here.
- **Solar power: 0.** Solar panels make nothing at all.
- **Day and night: 3 minutes.** A brief flash of light, the rest is night.

The Core's pressure is 200, lower than any vanilla planet (Aquilo is 300). So a
limit of 1 to 299 means the Core alone, and the mod's Core-only buildings and
recipes use it. Fire works here: boilers, furnaces and burner machines need
only 10. So do roboports, and the cryogenic plant and fusion can be made.
Anything that needs 300 or more is refused.

## 2. Resources

Five resources found on the core.

**Kamacite ore:** few, very rich patches, far apart, that run out. One patch of
about 120,000 lies near the landing, and the boulders give a little by hand.
Only the Ballast Drill mines it.

**Kamacite vent:** molten kamacite, a fluid that never runs out but slows as it
is drawn, as crude oil does. It can't be barrelled, so it never leaves the
Core. The Vent Pump draws it, and needs helium-3 to do so. About 11 vents lie
together near the landing.

**Helium-3 vent:** a gas. It never runs out, but gives less the more it is
used. It can't be put in barrels. It is rarer than the kamacite vent. The Gas
Pump draws it. About 11 vents are near the landing.

**Hydrogen vent:** a gas that burns. It never runs out, but gives less the more
it is used. It can't be put in barrels. Its wells stand alone and far apart,
like crude oil. The Gas Pump draws it. Four wells are near the landing.

**Radiant pool:** lakes of radiant solution, a liquid that never runs out. They
cover between 6% and 30% of the ground, depending on the map. A vanilla
offshore pump draws it from the shore. A small pool lies near the landing.

## 3. Why the Core can't do it all itself

Each planet's tree ends in a product that can only be made on that planet,
because it needs something only that planet has. The Core's final line needs
all five, so the Core can never do without them.

## 4. The line spans surface and orbit

Part of the final line is made on a space platform above the Core. The Core
has the strongest gravity in the game and the platform has none, and the line
needs both. So the player runs one factory in two places.
