# Vision

Space Age Extended is an optional layer over Factorio: Space Age. It adds
nothing to the vanilla path and takes nothing away from it; a player who ignores
the mod finishes the game exactly as they would have.

## The gap it fills

Vanilla Space Age gives every planet its own self-contained tech tree. A player
can finish the game treating Nauvis, Vulcanus, Fulgora, Gleba and Aquilo as five
separate factories connected only by rockets and cargo pods. Nothing on one
world genuinely needs anything from another; the planets are destinations, not
trading partners.

Two things follow from that. The planets never develop a relationship, and space
platforms — the one context every player eventually shares — stay generic, since
platform technology is the same regardless of which worlds you have visited.

## The claim

> **Every planet pair can have an industrial relationship worth building for, and
> the sum of those relationships becomes a destination.**

Each pair that gets a tree gains a genuine two-way dependency: a recipe on one
world that cannot be completed without a material that exists only on the other.
Each tree terminates in one space-platform capability. The accumulated
capabilities are what make the mod's own destination reachable.

## The destination

Vanilla leaves a sentence unfinished. The Shattered Planet is not a place you can
go — you travel *toward* it, take escalating damage, and eventually turn back or
die. Halfway out the useful asteroids stop and are replaced by promethium, which
crushes into nothing, so a flying factory can no longer feed itself.

The mod finishes it. The Shattered Planet has a **Core** — the intact metallic
heart of the destroyed world — still out there past the point where every vanilla
platform turns around. Reaching it means building and maintaining a **supply
corridor**: a chain of parked, defended, self-powering depots that refine local
promethium into the consumables the run needs.

The elegant part is what the corridor is made of. The defining fact out there is
that the only available material is useless. The mod's answer is to make that
material the fuel, the ammunition and the propellant. **You survive the
promethium field by eating it.**

The Core itself is the first world in the game that cannot be bootstrapped: metal
and vacuum, no water, no oil, no biology, no lava, no oxygen, so nothing burns.
Everything arrives down the corridor. That inverts the arrival ritual the player
has performed four times already, and makes the corridor the point rather than
the ceremony.

## What it is not

- **Not a rebalance.** No vanilla recipe, building or objective changes.
- **Not a sixth planetary tech tree.** The Core adds no science pack and no local
  material economy of its own.
- **Not flat bonuses.** A tree that grants "+15% when using imported material"
  creates no factory-design problem, which is the entire goal.
- **Not a new win condition.** The Solar System Edge stays the game's ending.
  Everything the mod adds past it is post-game, and the mod may require its own
  content only for its own destination.

## The test

Any proposed addition, at any scale, has to answer one question:

> Does it create a production decision the player did not have before, anchored
> on a resource that genuinely cannot be replaced by moving a building?

If not, it does not belong in the mod, however much content it would add.
