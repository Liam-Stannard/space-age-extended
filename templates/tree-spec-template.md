# <Planet>'s tree

**Tree spec template.** Copy to `design/trees/<planet>.md` and fill it in. What
every tree must add is in `design/01-principles.md` (principles 3, 5 and 7) and
`design/00-vision.md`.

Status:

1. The planet: what the tree adds to its vanilla tree
2. When it opens
3. The new resource
4. The chain: recipes, buildings, byproducts
5. The problem it gives the player (principle 5)
6. The platform item (principle 3)
7. The capstone: product and building
8. Technologies: each under its own heading, with its description, cost, purpose and dependencies
9. Getting the product to the Core (principle 7)
10. Open questions
