# Option E — the Plate Stack

**One line.** A low stack of insulator discs with the charge climbing the gaps between them, so what lights is the spaces rather than the object.

**What moves:** the gaps between the discs, lighting one after another up the stack — roughly
**2.4 tiles across and most of the height, about 70 % of the box**.

## The idea

Light the spaces, not the thing.

A short **stack of discs** sits on a broad plinth: five or six heavy poured
plates, each a little smaller than the one below, separated by narrow gaps. The
stack stands about a tile and a quarter and is capped by a solid dark disc that is
plainly the end of it.

When the machine charges, the **gaps light** — one after another from the bottom
up, warm white, the light escaping sideways between the plates. The plates
themselves stay dark: they are insulators, and what is between them is the
charge.

The bare inlet enters the plinth at the south tile edge.

## Why it suits this machine

**A stack of insulators is the one piece of electrical vocabulary that means
"holding apart" rather than "reaching out".** It says high voltage without saying
antenna, which is the exact needle this brief is trying to thread.

**Light climbing a stack is a progress bar the player reads without being
taught.** Four seconds of craft, five gaps: the building tells you how far
through it is, which nothing else in the round does.

**And the plates give it a silhouette with texture.** Every other option here is
one smooth mass; this is the only one whose black shape has steps in it, which is
what survives at map zoom.

## What it risks

**A stack of discs on a plinth is a transformer bushing, and a bushing is
electrical infrastructure that gets struck.** This is the option closest to the
anti-read and it knows it: the defence is that it is wide, short and capped, but
if a generator stretches it the building becomes an insulator column on a
substation.

**Narrow lit gaps are the first thing to vanish at 32 px.** The whole animation
lives in slots a pixel or two tall on screen, which is the Dross Classifier's
mistake in a different costume.

**And "the plates are dark and the gaps are lit" is a hard instruction to
follow.** The likeliest failure is a sheet where the discs glow at their edges,
which inverts the idea and makes the insulators look like the emitters.

## Concept Sheet Prompt

```text
FACTORIO SPACE AGE BUILDING -- CONCEPT SHEET

== CAMERA ==
Match the attached vanilla sprites exactly: looking steeply down from above,
MOSTLY ROOF with only a shallow near face visible. Square to the tile grid --
the near face parallel to the bottom edge of the frame, the side faces parallel
to the left and right edges. The square base must read as a SQUARE, never as a
diamond or a rhombus. Not rotated corner-on. Not a flat front elevation.
The attached vanilla sprites are STYLE references only: match the camera,
rendering, finish, LEVEL OF DETAIL and COLOUR TEMPERATURE; do NOT copy the
design, shape or components. The attached sheet of our own is a reference for
FINISH AND FAMILY -- this machine and the one in it are one installation -- but
the machine in it is a different, much larger building and this one must have
its own shape.

== BUILDING ==
Ignition Ring Mast -- option E, the Plate Stack.
A THREE-BY-THREE charging post on an airless metal world. Helium is pumped into
it, it holds a charge, and it hands the charge on as an item. It is a late,
exotic machine and it stands beside the Ignition Array.

== FORM ==
- A BROAD LOW PLINTH filling the 3x3, poured and seamless
- On it a SHORT STACK of FIVE OR SIX heavy DISCS, each a little smaller than the
  one below, separated by NARROW GAPS. The stack stands about a tile and a
  quarter -- WIDE AND SHORT, never a column
- A SOLID DARK CAP DISC closing the top, plainly the end of the stack: no finial,
  no rod, no terminal, nothing protruding
- The DISCS THEMSELVES ARE DARK and matte -- they are insulators. The LIGHT IS IN
  THE GAPS BETWEEN THEM, warm white, escaping sideways. The disc edges do NOT
  glow
- A PLAIN HELIUM INLET entering the plinth at the SOUTH tile edge, bare machine
  metal, square to the edge
- A small round white STATUS LENS on the plinth's near face
- Smooth dense material throughout, deep soft bevels, no fasteners anywhere

== COLOUR ==
- Plinth and discs: #4A463F to #6E685C, dark warm iron-nickel, the discs matte
  and noticeably darker than the plinth
- Gap collars: #8A5A32 to #C88A4A, copper and brass, unlit as material
- Charge light: #F0E0A8 to #FFF8E0, warm white, INSIDE the band only
- Emitter cap: #3B3B40, dark, closed, and never lit
- Status lens: #FFFFFF, painted white
- Copper and brass: #8A5A32 to #C88A4A, VISIBLE and used freely on the band's
  windings and its collars. This is what keeps the building warm. Do not ration
  it -- but it is a MATERIAL and never glows.

== RULES ==
- REGISTER: TIER 4, the endgame. EXOTIC, barely reading as machinery: monolithic
  surfaces, NO SEAMS ANYWHERE, NO RIVETS, NO BOLTS, NO FASTENERS OF ANY KIND, and
  contained light doing the work. Smooth, dense, poured-looking material. It must
  NOT look like it came from the same era as a riveted tier-0 machine.
- THE ANTI-READ IS A LIGHTNING COLLECTOR, and this is the most important rule
  here. NO CAGE. NO OPEN ELECTRODE. NO ANTENNA, MAST, SPIRE, ROD, PYLON OR
  FINIAL. NOTHING REACHING UPWARD. NOTHING AT THE TOP THAT LOOKS LIKE IT WANTS TO
  BE STRUCK. The top of this machine is CLOSED, BLUNT AND DARK.
- THE LIGHT IS CONTAINED AND WARM WHITE. #F0E0A8 to #FFF8E0, and it lives INSIDE
  the charge band -- glowing through the material rather than shining out of a
  hole. NOT violet, NOT green, NOT blue: those belong to other buildings of ours.
- THE TOP STAYS DARK. No light at the emitter, ever, in any panel. If the top
  glows, the building is telling the player the opposite of what it does.
- EXACTLY ONE FLUID CONNECTION: the helium inlet on the SOUTH face, centre tile.
  It runs down to GROUND LEVEL and stops flush at the middle of that tile edge,
  SQUARE to the face and pointing STRAIGHT out of it. Axis-aligned, never
  diagonal, never out of a corner, never elbowed across a shoulder. It does not
  float clear of the building and it does not stop short inside it.
- THE FLUID CONNECTIONS THEMSELVES ARE BARE. Every flange, stub and port is
  plain machine metal: no frost, no rime, no ice, no heat glow, no scorch, no
  lagging, no colour treatment of any kind. Whatever the fluid does to the
  body of the building, it STOPS SHORT of the connection. The engine caps an
  unconnected port with its own neutral flange, and a treated stub would not
  match it.
- A small round white STATUS LENS on the mass, well clear of the charge band, so
  the two lights are never confused. It is PAINTED WHITE in the hero view, the
  top-down view, the detail panels and the silhouette -- the engine colours it in
  game -- and drawn coloured ONLY inside the three-state row.
- Draw NO product anywhere: no charge, no cell, no canister, no cargo. Every port
  and recess is drawn EMPTY.
- Nothing extends sideways past the 3x3 footprint, the inlet included. Height
  above it is fine; width is not. No visible electrical connector -- power arrives
  from a pole off-frame.
- Airless metallic world: no flame, no exhaust, no steam, no smoke, no dust, no
  arcs, no sparks, no lightning, no plasma, no electrical discharge of any kind
  anywhere on or around this building.
- Not generic science fiction: no neon strip lighting, no holograms, no decals,
  no hazard chevrons, no glowing runes or panel lines.
- FORBIDDEN READS: it must NOT read as a lightning rod, a tesla coil, an antenna,
  a radio mast, a pylon or a lamp post. Not a reactor and not a turret.

== PANELS ==
- A large hero view of the whole building
- A silhouette panel: the same building as a flat black shape on grey, to prove
  it is readable with no detail at all
- A top-down view from directly overhead, WITHOUT grid lines drawn on it
- Two close-up detail panels: two discs with a lit gap between them, the disc faces dark and matte; and the solid cap disc closing the top of the stack
- A SOUTH-FACE view showing the bare helium inlet meeting the tile edge square-on
- A FOUR-FRAME MOTION ROW of the same view: all gaps dark; the lowest gap lit; three gaps lit climbing the stack; every gap lit and holding. The cap disc is DARK in all four
- A THREE-STATE ROW of the same view, three frames: RUNNING with the charge band
  lit warm white and the status lens GREEN; BLOCKED with the band dark and the
  lens AMBER; IDLE with the band dark, the emitter dark and the lens out. In all
  three the top of the machine is DARK
- A palette strip of eight colour swatches

== OUTPUT ==
One landscape concept sheet, 3:2, labelled panels on a dark charcoal background,
in the style of a game art bible page. Title the sheet IGNITION RING MAST -- PLATE STACK --
use exactly that name, do not invent a generic descriptive title. Panel labels
only; no other text anywhere on the sheet. No logos, wordmarks or watermarks.
```
