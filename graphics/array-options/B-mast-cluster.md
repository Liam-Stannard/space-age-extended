# Option B — the Mast Cluster

**One line.** A braced deck with four arc masts standing at its corners over a
central discharge electrode, charging the way a storm charges.

## The idea

The Array's recipe contains **4 arc masts**. Option B takes that literally: the
building is the four masts, tied together by a deck and a capacitor bank, aimed
at a single electrode in the middle. It is the only option that looks like it
belongs to the Core's weather rather than to its factory.

It also reframes what the Array *is*. Not a launch pad and not a reactor — a
lightning rod pointed the wrong way, throwing the storm back down.

## Why it suits the Core

Arc storms are the Core's defining hazard and its power source, and the mast is
already the planet's signature object. Reusing it at the end says "everything
you learned here, at scale" more directly than any new form could. Four masts on
a 9 × 9 also solves the height problem honestly: they are at the corners, so the
tall parts are where the footprint is otherwise wasted.

## Massing and palette

A low square deck, thick and anchored, with four heavy masts rising at the
corners — squat and guyed, not slender. Between them a capacitor bank and the
electrode assembly. Steel and ceramic insulator stacks, copper terminals,
kamacite plate. The masts are the only vertical thing in the composition and the
deck is deliberately plain so they read.

## The charge display

**Four insulator stacks, filling base to tip.** Each mast's stack is a column of
ceramic discs. Charge lights them from the bottom up, all four in step, so the
building has four identical gauges at its corners readable from any angle — the
one option where the display cannot be hidden behind the building's own mass.

At 100% all four stacks are full and the electrode between them starts to arc.

## Plates

`base`, `charge-overlay` (four stacks lit full height, masked by fill level),
`shadow`. Three, and no shaft plate at all — the electrode sits on the deck
rather than over a hole.

## Cost and risk

A fresh design, so a full generation round. Two risks. Four masts at the corners
is a lot of vertical in a 9 × 9, and the whole thing may scale down until the
deck reads as small. And it may look like a bigger arc mast rather than like the
final building — the mod's most-seen object, repeated.

## Concept Sheet Prompt

```text
FACTORIO SPACE AGE BUILDING -- CONCEPT SHEET

== CAMERA ==
Match the attached vanilla sprites exactly: steeply down from above, MOSTLY ROOF
with only a shallow near face. Square to the tile grid. Not rotated corner-on.
Not a flat front elevation.
The four attached sprites are style references only: match the camera, rendering,
finish and level of detail; do NOT copy the design, shape, colours or components.

== BUILDING ==
Ignition Array - option B, Mast Cluster.
A low square armoured deck with four squat lightning masts at its
corners and one discharge electrode at its centre. 9x9 tiles.

== FORM ==
- A low, thick, square deck, anchored at its edges
- Four braced lattice masts, one at each corner, squat and guyed, not slender
- Each mast is about half the width of the deck in height, no more
- Each mast carries a stack of ceramic insulator discs and a copper terminal
- Banks of capacitors and heavy cabling between the masts on the deck
- One thick discharge electrode assembly at the centre of the deck, aimed down
  into the ground

== COLOUR ==
- Body casing: #4A463F to #6E685C, warm low-value iron-nickel, old dark cast iron
- Pale nickel-white: #B9B4A8, confined to bolt rings and end caps
- Ceramic: #C9C0AC, the lightest value on the building
- Copper: #8A5A32, confined to busbars and straps
- Charge: violet-white #C9B6FF ramping to #FFFFFF, in the charge frames only
- NOT pale galvanised or bleached weathered steel. That is the default drift and
  it must be argued down. Warm dark desaturated steel, so violet is the only
  saturated colour on the sheet.

== RULES ==
- Airless metallic world, gravity 50: squat, thick, anchored. Nothing cantilevers,
  nothing is delicate, nothing is slender.
- Nothing burns here. No flame, no exhaust, no steam, no smoke, no fire, anywhere
  on the sheet, in any panel.
- Superconducting, therefore cold. Frost and rime are correct; glowing hot metal
  is wrong.
- Nothing glows at rest. Violet appears only in the charge frames.
- The building sits inside a 9x9 tile square with no part crossing the edge.
- Must read as one machine with four masts, not as four separate arc masts
  standing near each other. The deck ties them into a single object.

== PANELS ==
- A large hero view of the building
- A tile-grid panel from directly overhead on a 9x9 tile grid, the building
  inside the grid with no part crossing the edge
- Three close-up detail panels: the top of one mast with its insulator disc stack and copper terminal;
the capacitor bank between two masts; the discharge electrode at the deck centre
- A row of five charge key frames labelled 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, showing
  each mast's stack of ceramic insulator discs filling with violet from
the bottom upward, all four in step, full to the tip at 100%
- A layer breakdown row: shadow, bare metal, charge overlay, discharge
- A palette strip of eight colour swatches

== OUTPUT ==
One landscape concept sheet, 3:2, labelled panels on a dark charcoal background,
in the style of a game art bible page. Panel labels only; no other text.
```
