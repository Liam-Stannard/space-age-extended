# Option D — the Busbar Yard

**One line.** No building at all — an open switchyard of capacitor banks and
copper busbars bolted to a plate, flat enough to see every part of.

## The idea

The other four options are objects. This one is *infrastructure*: rows of
capacitor cans, disconnect switches, and heavy copper bus running between them,
laid out on an anchored deck with walkways. It is the least dramatic and the
most factory-like, and it is the only option that looks like something the
player built rather than something they found.

Flat also means the 9 × 9 problem disappears. Nothing is tall, so nothing gets
scaled down, and the footprint is filled edge to edge.

## Why it suits the Core

Dumping 50 MW into a crust is a switchgear problem before it is anything else,
and the Core has no weather to protect the gear from — no rain, no atmosphere,
so an open yard is *correct* here in a way it would be absurd on Nauvis. The
building's honesty is the point: this is what a machine that stores a colossal
charge actually looks like.

## Massing and palette

Flat, orthogonal, dense. Rows of upright capacitor cylinders in steel frames,
copper bus overhead on short standoffs, cable trenches, a walkway grid. No
silhouette to speak of — the interest is in the density and the repetition.
Copper, grey steel, kamacite plate, rust streaks.

## The charge display

**Rows filling across the deck, like a bar.** The capacitor cans light violet
row by row from one side of the yard to the other. Because the yard is flat and
regular, the boundary between charged and uncharged rows is a clean line the eye
reads instantly, and the display is the largest of any option — it is the whole
building.

At 100% every can is lit and the bus between them glows.

## Plates

`base`, `charge-overlay` (all cans lit, masked by a moving boundary), `shadow`.
Three, and the mask is a straight edge — the simplest overlay of the five.

## Cost and risk

Cheap to draw and cheap to light. The risk is that it does not feel like an
ending. It is the least monumental option by a distance, and the last building
in a mod arguably has to be an event. There is also no bore, so the rocket rises
out of the middle of a flat yard with nothing to rise from.
