# Option D — the Coil Trap

**One line.** A heavy helical exchanger wound round a central core, warm at its foot and frosted at its crown, with the split written as a thread rather than a line.

## The idea

Put the process on the outside of the machine as a shape. A short, fat, sealed
**core** stands in the middle of the footprint, and wound round it — outside the
shell, in plain view — is a single continuous **helical pipe**, four or five
turns of heavy lagged duct climbing from the core's base to its shoulder. The
melt goes in at the bottom of the coil and works its way up; the gas comes off at
the top.

The lowest turn is dark with dull orange in its lagging joints. The turns get
paler as they climb, and the top two are frost-jacketed. Between them the
transition is not a smooth fade but a **turn boundary**: one turn is warm, the
next is cold, and the clamp between them is where it happens.

## Why it suits this machine

**It is the only option whose silhouette is a texture.** Four heavy turns of pipe
round a drum reads as a screw thread or a spring at any zoom — a shape no other
machine on this floor has, and one that survives the silhouette panel completely.
The Coil Separator's fin stack is the nearest thing and it is a flat bank, not a
spiral.

**It explains the residence time**, which is the actual mechanic. §3.1 says the
building's entire mass exists to hold the melt still long enough for gas to come
out of solution. A long coiled path is that sentence drawn: the melt has a
distance to travel, and the machine is the distance.

**The split reads at four points instead of one.** A gradient up a coil is more
information than a line round a drum, and it is legible even when the building is
partly occluded by a neighbour.

## What it risks

**It is exposed pipework on a tier 3 building**, and the tier register asks for
machinery *implied rather than shown*. This is the option that most obviously
fights its own tier, and the answer has to be finish rather than concealment:
the coil is a smooth lagged chamfered duct with clamped collars, not a bare bent
tube with visible weld beads. If the sheet comes back looking like a still, the
option has failed.

**A coil round a drum is a still.** Also a heat exchanger, a condenser and a
worm — and one of those is a *distillation* apparatus, which is the anti-read
§3.1 names. The defences are that it is squat, that it has no receiver vessel,
and that the coil is heavy structural duct rather than thin tubing. Those have to
be prominent.

**It is a hard shape to keep inside 3 x 3.** A helix wants to be drawn generously
and the outermost turn is what crosses the footprint edge first. The prompt
states the width limit twice.

Helium Concentrator -- option D, the Coil Trap.

  It is heavy structural lagged duct with clamped collars at each turn -- smooth,
  chamfered and machined, never thin bare tubing and never a bent tube with weld
  beads showing.
- The turns change temperature as they climb, at TURN BOUNDARIES rather than by
  fading: the lowest turn or two are dark with dull orange incandescence in

- Far face, centre: a slim pale frost-jacketed GAS OUTLET leaving the TOP of the

  ground level
- Left face, centre: a HEAVY INSULATED outlet line leaving the core's skirt low,
  at ground level

== COLOUR ==
- Body, core and coil: #4A463F to #6E685C, warm low-value iron-nickel, sealed
  and chamfered
- Melt heat and melt lines: #7A4A28 to #C86A2E, in the LOWEST TURNS' lagging
  joints only, dull and contained
- Frost and gas pipe: #BFD8E8 to #EAF4FA, on the top turns and the gas pipe
- Shielding collars: #3B3B40, near-black, clamped at all three ports and at each

- Copper and brass: #8A5A32 to #C88A4A, VISIBLE and used freely on bus runs,
  joints, flange faces, turn collars and fittings. This is what keeps the
  building warm. Do not ration it.
- WARM metal, a COLD crown and a HOT foot. The shell must not drift blue.

== RULES ==
- REGISTER: TIER 3, the Core's own goods -- advanced, quiet, sealed. Seamless welded shells, chamfered forms, no visible fasteners on the primary faces, cryogenic jacketing where it is earned, machinery implied rather than shown. No rivets, no rust, no exposed gears or linkages. The coil is EXPOSED but it is finished to that standard: lagged, chamfered and clamped, not raw pipework. But it is HEAVY and SHORT: it stands on the heaviest world in the game, so it is squat and dense, never tall.
- Draw NO loose material anywhere: no ore, powder, grit, melt, gas, debris or
  product on the ground, at ports, at flanges, in trays, or spilling from the
  machine. Factorio machines never show what they make, so every port, flange
  and opening is drawn as EMPTY machinery.
- The working chamber is enclosed. The core is sealed and the coil is a closed
  duct: no open melt surface, no window, nothing visibly flowing.
- EXACTLY THREE FLUID CONNECTIONS TO THE OUTSIDE WORLD and no more, one on each
  of three different faces, as listed above. The helical coil is part of the
  machine and does not count and must not reach an edge. Each outside connection
  runs down to GROUND LEVEL and stops flush at the middle of its tile edge,
  square to that face. Axis-aligned, never diagonal. No pipe leaves the roof, no
  pipe stops in mid-air, no pipe crosses the edge of the footprint. Each flange
  is drawn into the building's own art.
- NO item ports of any kind: this machine moves only fluids. No chutes, no bins,
  no hatches aimed at a tile. No visible electrical connector either -- power
  arrives from a pole off-frame.
- Nothing extends sideways past the 3x3 footprint, pipework included. THE
  OUTERMOST TURN OF THE COIL SITS INSIDE IT. Height above it is fine; width is
  not.
- Airless metallic world, gravity 50: nothing burns and nothing blows about.
  No flame, no exhaust, no steam, no smoke, no fire, no dust cloud, no vapour
  plume, anywhere.
- HOT IS NOT FIRE. The heat in the lowest turns is INCANDESCENT METAL seen
  through narrow lagging joints, and conducted heat glowing dull in seams. It
  comes from inside and it is contained. No flame, no combustion, no furnace
  mouth.
- THE TEMPERATURE SPLIT HAPPENS AT A TURN BOUNDARY, not as a fade: warm turns
  below, cold turns above, one clamped collar between them. Copper and brass are
  a MATERIAL and never glow; the only emissive orange on this building is the
  melt heat in the lowest turns.
- NO GREEN anywhere. Green belongs to another material in this mod and this gas
  is not that.
- Not generic science fiction: no neon strip lighting, no holograms, no decals,
  no hazard chevrons used as decoration.
- FORBIDDEN READS: it must NOT read as a distillation column, a refinery tower
  or a STILL -- no fractionating trays, no ladder cage, no flare stack, no
  receiver vessel, no thin copper worm. It must not read as a spring, a coiled
  hose or a cable drum: the coil is heavy lagged structural duct with clamped
  collars, and the core it wraps is plainly a sealed machine.
