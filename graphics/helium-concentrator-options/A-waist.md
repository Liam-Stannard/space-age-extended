# Option A — the Waist

**One line.** One fat sealed drum filling the footprint, with a machined belt at mid-height: hot below it, frosted above it.

## The idea

Take §3.1 at its word and draw it once, properly. A single heavy separation drum
occupies almost the whole 3 x 3, ribbed horizontally, standing on a low plinth.
At exactly half its height a machined belt runs right round it — a raised
chamfered band, thicker than the ribs, with the frost jacketing beginning on its
upper edge and stopping dead on its lower one. Below the belt the shell is dark
and its slot joints glow dull orange; above it the shell is pale and dry-frosted.

The gas riser climbs the far flank from the belt, and comes back down to the far
tile edge. The melt return leaves low on the left. The inlet is a wide dark
flange on the near face.

This is the conventional answer, carried as a fair comparison rather than as the
favourite. If none of the other four beats it, that is worth knowing.

## Why it suits this machine

**The temperature split is the silhouette, not a decal on it.** The belt is a
physical object that can be seen at map zoom, and the two halves of the drum are
different colours on either side of it. No other option puts the line that
plainly.

It is also the only option where the read survives being small. A drum with a
band is legible at 32 px, which matters because §16 asks the icon to say *"gas
coming off metal"* rather than *"a tank"*, and the icon is drawn from whatever
wins here.

## What it risks

**It is a tank with a stripe on it.** The silhouette from above is a circle
inside a square, and a circle inside a square is a storage tank, a fluid tank or a
silo — three things this building is not. The ribs and the three flanges are the
only argument against that, and ribs disappear first at distance.

**It is the closest of the five to the distillation column §3.1 forbids.** A
ribbed drum with a slim pipe climbing one flank is a refinery tower drawn short,
and the only thing keeping it from being one is that it stays squat. If the sheet
comes back tall, the option has failed on its own terms.

**It is also the closest to the Coil Separator**, which is now locked as a
jacketed vessel with frost collars standing on the same factory floor. Both are
tier 3, both are pale-jacketed, both are round. That comparison has to be made
before this one is picked.
