# Option B — the Cold Cap

**One line.** A wide low holding pan taking up the whole footprint, carrying one small frost-jacketed condenser bell — nine tenths hot mass, one tenth cold.

## The idea

Draw the proportion instead of the process. Almost the entire building is a
sealed rectangular **holding pan**: a heavy, dark, ribbed slab that fills the
3 x 3 and stands only about a third of a tile tall at its shoulder, with melt
lines running along its flanks and dull orange in the joints between its plates.
Sitting on the roof of that slab, off-centre and quite small, is a single
frost-jacketed **condenser bell** — a chamfered dome perhaps a third of the
footprint across, pale and dry, with the gas riser leaving its crown.

The joke is the same one that won the Coil Separator: all that mass, to get that
little out of it. Here it is the *right* joke, because §2 says so in numbers —
100 melt in, 10 gas out.

## Why it suits this machine

**It draws the trade.** §2's recipe is deliberately a bad exchange, and this is
the only option where the arithmetic is visible in the shape: the hot part is
enormous and the cold part is a cap on it. A player who has never read the
tooltip can see that most of what goes in stays in.

**It is roof-dominant, which is where the camera looks.** A 3 x 3 machine seen
from Factorio's angle is mostly roof, and this design puts its one distinctive
feature — a pale bell on a dark deck — exactly there. Option A's belt is on the
flanks, where the camera sees least of it.

**The temperature split is between two objects rather than across one**, which is
a cheaper read at distance: dark slab, pale cap.

## What it risks

**A low slab with a dome on it is the Sealed Roboport.** That building is already
on the Core, it is already a dome, and the Whisker Comber was picked partly on
the promise of staying clear of it. The defences are proportion — the Roboport's
dome is most of the building, this bell is a fifth of it — and finish, since this
one is jacketed, chamfered and ribbed rather than smooth. Both have to survive
stage 1.

**A wide low box is also every other machine on this floor.** The Dross
Classifier is a stepped box; the Drop Crusher is a box. If the bell is drawn
small enough to be honest about the ratio, it may be too small to separate the
building from them at map zoom, and the silhouette panel is where that gets
decided.

**The hot mass has nowhere to hide.** A slab with orange in its plate joints,
drawn wrong, becomes a hotplate or a hearth — an open heat source, which is
precisely the thing an airless world does not have.

== COLOUR ==

  joints, flange faces and fittings. This is what keeps the building warm.

Seamless welded shells, chamfered forms, no visible fasteners on the primary faces, cryogenic jacketing where it is earned, machinery implied rather than shown. No rivets, no rust, no exposed gears or linkages. But it is HEAVY and SHORT: it stands on the heaviest world in the game and holds molten metal still, so it is squat and dense, never tall.
- Draw NO loose material anywhere: no ore, powder, grit, melt, gas, debris or

  and opening is drawn as EMPTY machinery.
- The working chamber is enclosed. The pan is SEALED -- it is a closed vessel
  seen from outside, never an open bath, hearth or hotplate, and no melt surface
  is visible anywhere.

  faces, as listed above. Each runs down to GROUND LEVEL and stops flush at the
  middle of its tile edge, square to that face. Axis-aligned, never diagonal.
  No pipe leaves the roof, no pipe stops in mid-air, no pipe crosses the edge of

  terminates at the edge. Each flange is drawn into the building's own art.

- Airless metallic world, gravity 50: nothing burns and nothing blows about.
  No flame, no exhaust, no steam, no smoke, no fire, no dust cloud, no vapour

- HOT IS NOT FIRE. The heat in the slab is INCANDESCENT METAL seen through
  narrow plate joints, and conducted heat glowing dull in seams. It comes from

- THE TEMPERATURE SPLIT IS BETWEEN THE TWO MASSES: dull emissive orange in the

- Not generic science fiction: no neon strip lighting, no holograms, no decals,

- FORBIDDEN READS: it must NOT read as a distillation column or a refinery tower
