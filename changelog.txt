Version: 0.2.1
Date: 2026-09-04
  Balancing:
    - Magmatic Core now has an explicit weight (1000, so 1000 per rocket). The engine's derived
      default was 100 -- the recipe is mostly Lava, and fluids weigh nothing -- which put 10,000
      cores in a rocket and made a rocket of them worth three times a rocket of fusion power cells.
      Shipping was effectively free, which defeated the point of a cross-planet fuel line. A rocket
      of cores is now 90GJ on the lean quench (roughly uranium parity) and 600GJ on the cryogenic
      quench (well under fusion's 2000GJ).
    - Catalyst Rod and Depleted Catalyst Rod weights set to 2000, and Copper Foil to 2000. Left
      derived, moving rods to Vulcanus and spent rods back cost twenty times moving the cores they
      exist to produce.
    - Radiative cooling now processes 40 coolant per craft instead of 10, so 2.5 radiators feed one
      quench plant instead of 10. At the old rate the cryogenic tier needed 80 tiles for 60MW and
      was *less* floor-space efficient than the lean tier it upgrades from -- backwards, on a
      platform, where floor space is the scarcest resource. It is now 50 tiles and 1.17 MW/tile,
      ahead of the lean tier and still behind fusion.
  Changes:
    - The platform coolant loop now runs on two new mod fluids, Quench Coolant and Spent Quench
      Coolant, instead of vanilla Fluoroketone. Vanilla deliberately provides no way to re-cool
      Fluoroketone in space (its cooling recipe is cryogenics-only, and a cryogenic plant cannot be
      built on a platform), so the previous vacuum-only Fluoroketone recipe had made this mod the
      only in-space source of fluoroketone-cold -- quietly removing vanilla fusion's coolant
      logistics for anyone running the mod. Aquilo is still the gate: Quench Coolant can only be
      made from Fluoroketone, in a cryogenic plant, on a planet.
    - Two fluids rather than one at two temperatures, because barrels do not preserve temperature:
      a single coolant could have been barrelled hot and emptied cold, laundering the radiator step
      away entirely.
    - Recipe "Radiative Fluoroketone Cooling" renamed to "Radiative Coolant Cooling"; migrated.
  Info:
    - The Quench Turbine's real advantage over nuclear is ice, not density: nuclear on a platform
      needs about 0.52 ice/s per MW to make water for its heat exchangers, against 0.067 for the
      cryogenic quench.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-03
  Major Features:
    - Quench Turbine: a space-platform generator burning Quench Vapour, replacing the Thermionic
      Generator. Magmatic Core is now an ingredient rather than a fuel: a quench recipe turns one
      core plus Ice into vapour at a temperature the recipe fixes, and the turbine clips anything
      hotter than it can use. Electricity per shipped core is therefore set by which quench recipe
      you can run, not by the item.
    - Lean Quench (unlocked with the turbine): 90MJ per core, most of it thrown away at the clip.
    - Cryogenic Quench (new technology, gated behind Aquilo's cryogenic plant and its Fluoroketone):
      600MJ per core, roughly seven times the lean recipe.
    - Radiative Fluoroketone Cooling: closes the Fluoroketone loop in space. A cryogenic plant
      cannot be built on a platform, so without this the Cryogenic Quench would stall on its own
      hot output. Vacuum-only, via a real surface condition.
  Changes:
    - The Thermionic Generator, its scripted temperature/efficiency curve, its two hidden paired
      entities and its custom GUI are all removed; control.lua is now empty. The old design could
      not survive vanilla's heat network, which would have converted its waste heat into roughly as
      much electricity again through heat exchangers and steam turbines.
    - Magmatic Core is no longer a fuel item and the sae-thermionic-fuel category is gone.
  Bugfixes:
    - none
  Info:
    - Migrating a save: stockpiled Thermionic Generators and any assembler set to its recipe become
      Quench Turbines. Generators already PLACED on a platform cannot be recovered -- the engine
      removes entities whose prototype is gone before any migration runs -- so the two Thermionic
      Assemblies each one cost are lost. Deconstruct them before updating if that matters.
